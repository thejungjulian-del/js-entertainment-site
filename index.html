◦ How the company works

Build. Operate. Own.

The group keeps the taste
The company keeps the machine

Build

Groups are assembled, not discovered. The aesthetic and the discipline are defined first, then people are cast into them — for how they fit the world, never for the audience they arrive with.

Fig. 02 — casting

Operate

Every group runs the same cycle: the shoot day, the release calendar, the deal desk, the numbers. Shared underneath, invisible on top. That is what makes the second group cheap.

Fig. 03 — the machine

Own

The group is the asset and it outlasts any line-up. The company owns the name and the work; members own their own faces and their own accounts.
